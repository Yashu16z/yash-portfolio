Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions about website sprints, tech stack, pricing, and project turnaround.

Why choose a custom coded sprint over WordPress or Wix templates? +
Custom-coded sites load in under 500ms, achieve 95-100 Google Lighthouse speed scores, and require zero monthly plugin maintenance fees or security vulnerabilities. Your site looks unique, ranks better on Google, and converts more visitors.
How fast will my website be delivered? +
One-page landing pages and conversion sprints (₹4,999) are delivered in 24 to 48 hours. Full multi-page business websites and AI tool prototypes (₹9,999) are typically delivered in 3 to 5 business days.
What do I need to prepare before we begin? +
Just a brief overview of your business/service, your logo (if you have one), and any sample websites you love. If you don't have copy or content ready, I can help generate conversion-focused draft copy for you using AI!
Will the website work seamlessly on smartphones and tablets? +
Yes, 100%. Every project is built mobile-first with adaptive CSS flexbox/grid, fluid typography, touch-friendly tap targets, and tested across iOS, Android, and desktop screen sizes.
How does payment work? +
For standard sprints, a 50% upfront deposit secures your sprint slot, and the remaining 50% is paid upon final staging approval right before domain launch. We accept UPI, Net Banking, and international wire/cards.
